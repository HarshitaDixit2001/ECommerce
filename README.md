# 🛒 Gen Softech – E-Commerce & Direct Billing Platform

## 🌐 Project Overview

**Shop.GensoftTech.com** is a modern **E-Commerce and Direct Billing platform** developed by **Gen Softech** for businesses that need an integrated online shopping, product management, customer management, and direct-selling ecosystem.

The platform is designed to support product-based businesses, FMCG companies, wellness brands, direct-selling organizations, and MLM businesses with a complete digital shopping experience.

The system combines:

- 🛍️ E-Commerce Website
- 🧾 Direct Billing
- 📦 Product Management
- 👥 Customer Management
- 💰 MRP / DP / BV Pricing
- 🛒 Shopping Cart

- 📱 Responsive Web Experience

# 💰 Direct Selling Pricing

The platform supports multiple pricing values suitable for direct-selling and MLM businesses.

### MRP

**Maximum Retail Price**

The standard retail price of a product.

### DP

**Distributor Price**

The price applicable to distributors, members, or eligible customers.

### BV

**Business Volume**

A business-volume value that can be used by MLM/direct-selling systems for calculating commissions, incentives, ranks, and other business rewards.
